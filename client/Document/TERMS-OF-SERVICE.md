# SmartServe - Terms of Service

**Last Updated: September 16, 2026**

Welcome to **SmartServe**, the campus canteen management, mobile ordering, and sustainability portal. By accessing or using our WebApp, digital cafeteria pass, mobile order services, or Eco Program, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

---

### 1. Acceptance of Terms & Eligibility
* **Eligibility**: Access to SmartServe is granted exclusively to enrolled students, faculty members, and authorized campus staff holding an active campus institutional identity.
* **Agreement**: By signing up, authenticating, scanning your Digital Campus Pass, or placing food orders, you confirm your adherence to campus canteen regulations, the Student Code of Conduct, and these terms.

---

### 2. User Accounts, Authentication & Security
* **Credential Safeguarding**: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials (password, authentication session). You may not share your account or authentication tokens with other individuals.
* **Network & Account Telemetry**: To protect campus user accounts and maintain system integrity, SmartServe logs login activity, originating client IP addresses, device form factors (mobile, tablet, desktop), and security failure telemetry. Unreasonable multi-device concurrent sessions or suspected unauthorized access may prompt temporary security holds.
* **Profile Accuracy**: Students and staff must provide accurate profile details (Full Name, School ID Number, Grade & Section or Department). Voluntarily uploaded profile photos must depict the account holder clearly for campus cashier identity verification.

---

### 3. Digital Campus Pass & QR Scanner Regulations
* **Digital Pass Ownership**: Your SmartServe Digital Campus Pass and its associated high-contrast QR identifier are personal to your registered student/employee profile.
* **Counter Verification**: Passes are scanned at cafeteria cashier counters and pickup stations for order claims, voucher redemptions, and BYOC container validations.
* **Anti-Tampering & Non-Transferability**: Selling, lending, distributing, generating counterfeit QR tokens, or sharing screenshots of digital passes to claim unauthorized orders or eco points is strictly prohibited and constitutes an academic honor violation.

---

### 4. Mobile Canteen Ordering & Kitchen Operations
* **Order Commitment**: Food orders submitted via the SmartServe mobile interface are dispatched to the cafeteria kitchen queue in real-time. Once kitchen preparation commences (**"Preparing"** status), orders cannot be cancelled or altered.
* **Shift Hours & Peak Rush Scheduling**: Meal ordering operates in synchronization with campus cafeteria operational shifts (Breakfast, Lunch Rush, Snack Periods). Real-time estimated wait times and kitchen rush counters are advisory and may fluctuate based on cafeteria counter volume.
* **Pick-up Window**: Users are notified when their order transitions to **"Ready for Pickup"**. Prepared meals must be claimed at the designated canteen pickup counter within 20 minutes of notification. Items unclaimed past the operational shift may be discarded without refund due to food safety and hygiene protocols.
* **Pricing & Stock Availability**: Menu pricing, nutritional designations, and item availability are managed in real-time by cafeteria administrators and are subject to immediate inventory adjustments.

---

### 5. Bring Your Own Container (BYOC) & Eco Rewards Program
* **Container Hygiene & Food Safety**: Students and faculty participating in the BYOC zero-waste initiative must present clean, dry, food-grade reusable containers at the cashier counter prior to food dispensing. Canteen staff reserve the right to decline unsuitable or unhygienic containers.
* **Eco Points Accrual**: Eco Points are credited following cashier verification upon scanning your Digital Campus Pass during a legitimate BYOC transaction.
* **Redemption Rules**: Eco Points may be redeemed solely for active items and vouchers listed in the official SmartServe Rewards Catalog. Eco Points possess no monetary or cash equivalent and cannot be transferred between accounts.
* **Fair Play**: Fraudulent attempts to claim BYOC points without providing qualifying reusable containers will result in immediate forfeiture of accrued eco points.

---

### 6. Acceptable Use & System Integrity
You agree not to engage in any of the following prohibited activities:
* **Automated Exploitation**: Utilizing bots, scrapers, automated scripts, or unauthorized browser extensions to place rapid orders or harvest system data.
* **Security & Network Tampering**: Attempting brute-force authentication, credential stuffing, IP address spoofing, unauthorized vulnerability scanning, or distributed denial of service (DDoS) against canteen infrastructure.
* **Order Queue Manipulation**: Submitting fictitious orders or intentionally creating fraudulent backlogs in the kitchen preparation queue.
* **Disruptive Submissions**: Submitting profane, abusive, or defamatory content through canteen feedback or support channels.

---

### 7. Administrative Discretion & Disciplinary Actions
Violation of these Terms of Service or campus dining policies may result in:
* Immediate order cancellation without reimbursement.
* Forfeiture of accrued Eco Points and catalog redemptions.
* Temporary suspension or permanent termination of your SmartServe digital canteen access.
* Referral of serious infractions (e.g. identity impersonation, system hacking, cashier harassment) to the Campus Student Affairs and Discipline Board.

---

### 8. Modifications to Service & Terms
SmartServe and campus cafeteria management reserve the right to revise menu structures, cafeteria shift hours, eco-incentive rates, and these Terms of Service to improve food service delivery and campus sustainability. Continued use of the portal after updates indicates acceptance of modified terms.
