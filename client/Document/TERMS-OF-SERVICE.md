**Last Updated: August 23, 2026**

---

### 1. Acceptance of Terms
By creating an account, logging in, or placing orders on SmartServe, you confirm that you are an enrolled student or authorized campus employee and agree to comply with campus canteen regulations and these terms.

---

### 2. User Accounts & Security
* **Account Credentials**: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials (password and authentication token).
* **QR Code Usage**: Your personal QR code is unique to your student/employee account. Sharing QR codes to claim unauthorized rewards or fraudulent orders is strictly prohibited.
* **Accuracy of Information**: Users must provide accurate profile details (Name, School ID, Grade/Section or Department).

---

### 3. Canteen Ordering & Payment Rules
* **Order Commitment**: Orders placed via SmartServe represent a binding request for food preparation at the canteen.
* **Order Status & Pick-up**: Students must collect their orders when status updates to **"Ready for Pickup"**. Orders not collected within the canteen operating window may be cancelled without refund.
* **Pricing & Modifications**: Menu items, prices, and availability are set by the school canteen management and are subject to real-time adjustments.

---

### 4. Bring Your Own Container (BYOC) & Eco Points Program
* **Container Hygiene**: Students participating in the BYOC Eco Program must present clean, food-safe reusable containers to canteen cashiers.
* **Points Accrual & Redemption**: Eco Points earned through BYOC scans can be redeemed exclusively for active items in the SmartServe Rewards Catalog. Eco Points cannot be exchanged for cash.
* **Fair Play**: Fraudulent attempts to obtain BYOC points without presenting a container will result in point forfeitures.

* **Respectful Communication**: When submitting feedback or communicating with canteen administrators, users must refrain from abusive, profane, or defamatory language.
* **Feedback Moderation**: Canteen management reserves the right to delete inappropriate submissions or disable accounts that violate campus conduct policies.
