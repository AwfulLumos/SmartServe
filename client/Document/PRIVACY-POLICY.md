# SmartServe - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 23, 2026**

SmartServe ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of students, faculty, and campus staff. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the SmartServe Canteen WebApp.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect only the essential data necessary to fulfill canteen orders, manage QR verifications, and operate the Eco Rewards Program:
* **Account Information**: Full Name, School ID Number, Email Address, Grade Level & Section (for students), or Job Title & Department (for employees).
* **Profile Media**: Profile pictures voluntarily uploaded by users.
* **Order & Transaction Data**: Food items ordered, payment total, order timestamps, and order status history.
* **Eco Program Data**: BYOC scan logs, Eco Points balance, and reward redemption history.
* **Support & Feedback Submissions**: Messages, categories, ratings, and feedback responses.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for internal campus canteen operations:
* Processing and preparing food orders efficiently.
* Validating student identity at the canteen cashier via QR code scans.
* Calculating Eco Points and awarding zero-waste incentives.
* Responding to student support inquiries and canteen feedback.
* Analyzing canteen demand and forecasting menu stock (in aggregated, anonymized formats).

---

### 3. Data Protection & Security
* **Authentication Security**: User passwords are encrypted using industry-standard salted hashing algorithms (bcrypt). Access tokens (JWT) are securely handled.
* **Restricted Access**: Access to personal student profiles is restricted strictly to authorized canteen administrators and system managers.
* **No Third-Party Data Selling**: SmartServe does NOT sell, rent, or share personal data with external advertising networks or commercial third parties.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
* **Active Accounts**: Profile data and order history are retained while your student or employee account remains active.
* **Account Deletion**: Students may request account deactivation or self-delete their submitted feedback records directly within the app. Soft-deleted accounts have personal records archived securely.

---

### 5. Cookies & Local Storage
SmartServe uses browser `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` solely to preserve authentication tokens, UI preferences (e.g. Dark Mode toggle, Notification settings), and active session state.

---

### 6. Updates to Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified within the SmartServe portal of any material changes.

---
