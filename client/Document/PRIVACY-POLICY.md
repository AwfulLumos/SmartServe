# SmartServe - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 16, 2026**

SmartServe ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of students, faculty, and campus staff. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you access the SmartServe Canteen WebApp, digital cafeteria pass, or administrative portals.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect only the essential data necessary to fulfill canteen orders, manage QR verifications, operate the Eco Rewards Program, and protect campus accounts from unauthorized access:
* **Account Information**: Full Name, School ID Number, Email Address, Grade Level & Section / Course (for students), or Job Title & Department (for cafeteria and administrative staff).
* **Profile Media**: Profile pictures voluntarily uploaded by students and staff, displayed on the SmartServe Digital Campus Pass and account header.
* **Order & Transaction Data**: Food items ordered, dietary customization notes, payment totals, hourly transaction timestamps, and real-time kitchen preparation status (pending, preparing, ready, completed).
* **Eco Program Data**: Bring Your Own Container (BYOC) scan logs, Eco Points balance, Eco Tier levels, and reward redemption history.
* **Network, Device & Security Telemetry**: Originating client IP addresses, device form factor (mobile phone, tablet, desktop workstation), browser user-agent signatures, campus network session timestamps, and regional geolocation ("Philippines") captured during authentication and portal access.
* **Security & Incident Telemetry**: Records of unsuccessful authentication attempts, failure reasons (e.g. incorrect password, account locked, unregistered account), timestamps, and origin network telemetry used to detect and prevent brute-force or unauthorized account access.
* **Support & Feedback Submissions**: Messages, categories, ratings, and feedback responses submitted to canteen management.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for internal campus canteen operations and system security:
* **Food Service Delivery**: Processing, scheduling, and preparing food orders efficiently through the cafeteria kitchen queue.
* **Identity & Voucher Verification**: Validating student identity at the canteen cashier via Digital Campus Pass QR code scans.
* **Sustainability & Rewards**: Calculating Eco Points, tracking reusable container usage, and awarding zero-waste incentives.
* **Account Security & Fraud Prevention**: Real-time security telemetry monitoring, detecting unauthorized login attempts, safeguarding user credentials, and maintaining administrative audit logs.
* **Cafeteria Operational Insights**: Analyzing rush hours, peak transaction periods, and inventory consumption trends in aggregated, anonymized formats to optimize food availability and reduce waste.
* **Student Support**: Responding to student inquiries, refund requests, and canteen feedback.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Security
* **Authentication Security**: User passwords are encrypted using industry-standard salted hashing algorithms (bcrypt). Sessions are authenticated using signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
* **Network Telemetry Protection**: IP address logs and failed login records are restricted exclusively to authorized system administrators for security auditing and incident response. Telemetry data is automatically pruned in accordance with campus retention schedules.
* **High-Contrast Digital Pass Security**: QR tokens generated for student passes contain secure, tamper-resistant identifiers verified only by authorized cafeteria terminal scanners.
* **No Third-Party Data Selling**: SmartServe does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share personal data with external advertising networks or commercial third parties.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
* **Active Accounts**: Profile data, transaction logs, and reward balances are retained while your student or employee account remains active.
* **Account Deletion & Archival**: Students may request account deactivation or self-delete submitted feedback records directly within the app. Deactivated accounts have personal records archived securely.
* **Security Telemetry Retention**: Failed authentication records and network logs are maintained for security auditing and may be cleared by authorized administrators once security reviews are concluded.

---

### 5. Cookies & Local Storage
SmartServe uses browser `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` solely to preserve authentication tokens, UI preferences (e.g. Dark Mode toggle, Notification preferences), and active session state. No third-party tracking cookies are deployed.

---

### 6. Updates to Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new system features, security protocols, or campus policies. Users will be notified within the SmartServe portal of any material revisions.
